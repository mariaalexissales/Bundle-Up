title=Bundle Up! - A Packing Mod
description=[h1]Bundle Up[/h1]
description=[h2]Ready for B42.20!![/h2]
description=[b]Meant to organize our excessive amounts of stuff![/b]
description=
description=Put 99 Nails in a box and now half your base is missing from server chunk rot? Got a shit ton of sheet metal spread among 20 crates because you just can’t fit it into one? No More!
description=
description=[b][i]Item weights for each bundle/box can be adjusted in the Sandbox Options menu if the defaults don’t suit your game :D[/i][/b]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What’s New[/h2]
description=
description=[b]New items:[/b] Coke (again, the blacksmithing kind) and gas cans are now packable!
description=
description=[b]Stacks go one level deeper.[/b] Cartons now pack into Cases, so a hoard that used to bottom out at “a shelf of cartons” collapses one more time.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Food Cases (4 cartons):[/b] all 166 food cartons, dry goods and produce alike. That’s 48 items in a single slot.
description=[*] [b]Magazine Cartons (12 boxes) & Cases (4 cartons):[/b] your spare B-F, JS-14, M16, M1911, M1A and M9 mags, up to 240 per case. Now whenever you siren pull, you can’t complain that you didn’t have enough mags to switch through.
description=[*] [b]Carton of Caps (12 boxes):[/b] caps were the only ammo in the game that never got a carton. Now they have one. [i][insert cheers here][/i]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Everything you drop is finally visible.[/b] 565 of the mod’s items had no world model and rendered as thin air when dropped on the ground. Every one of the 780 items now has one. I want to see the most impressive pile of bundled items.
description=
description=[b]Rope bundles look like rope bundles*.[/b] Tied plank, sheet metal, pipe and bar bundles used to render as a single loose plank. They’re now actual strapped bundles, and the 10-count ones look bigger than the 5-count ones.
description=
description=[b]Perishable Timer:[/b] Food keeps spoiling while it’s packed away, so a carton is storage, not a stasis pod - and that now holds however deep you stack it. A packed carton is treated as food in its own right, so it chills in a fridge, freezes solid in a freezer and thaws out again on exactly the same schedule as the loose food beside it, and a frozen carton shows the same frost you’d see on anything else. Age carries all the way up and down the stack, so a case of cartons ticks exactly like a lone carton does, and a rotten carton can’t be laundered into a fresh-looking case. Rotten food can’t be packed in the first place, and the spoilage rate is adjustable in Sandbox Options (set it to 0% if you’d rather cartons preserve).
description=
description=[quote][b]You can’t eat a carton[/b], only open it. [b]PLEASE DO NOT ATTEMPT TO EAT THE CARTON.[/b] Do note that anywhere you’d already explored before updating keeps its old cartons as they were.[/quote]
description=
description=[b]New loot:[/b] magazine cartons turn up in gun stores, army and SWAT storage at about a quarter the rate of the boxes, and cap cartons in the Wild West sheriff furniture that already carried the boxes.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] cartons of non-perishable food (granola bars, chocolate, crisps, cookies, dry goods) threw a Lua error every time the crafting window refreshed, and couldn’t be packed into a Case at all. Both sorted.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] the Sandbox spoilage rate only ever slowed the carton down, never what came out of it - so at 0% a carton would sit there looking perfectly fresh and still unpack into a pile of rotten food. Packing and unpacking now carry how much shelf life is left rather than a raw day count, so 0% really does hold food exactly as you packed it, and 50% really does make it last twice as long. Changing the setting also applies to cartons you already have instead of only new ones.
description=
description=[b]Changed:[/b] packing now saves weight, not just slots. A pack weighs five sixths of what it holds, which is exactly what vanilla charges for a carton of eggs or a box of canned food, and boxes of nails and screws weigh 40% of theirs to match vanilla’s own box of 100. Every pack in the mod also declares that weight up front now instead of a placeholder, so what the item says and what your inventory says finally agree. All of it is still adjustable per category and per item in Sandbox Options.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] a spoiled or frozen carton could not be opened at all - the game refuses rotten and frozen crafting inputs unless a recipe says otherwise, and the unpack recipes never did. You can now open a rotten carton and get rotten food out of it, and open a frozen one and get frozen food out of it. Packing rotten food is still refused, same as vanilla.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] the Nuts & Bolts box held 50 but was weighed as though it held 10, so it carried a fifth of its real load. It also sat in the Cooking category instead of Material.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] the sacks of coke, the six-packs of cola and diet cola, and the roped gas can bundles were missing from the weight table entirely, so they kept a placeholder weight and ignored the Sandbox reduction sliders. All six now weigh what they hold and have their own per-item slider.
