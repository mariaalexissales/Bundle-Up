title=Bundle Up! - A Packing Mod
description=[h1]Bundle Up[/h1]
description=[h2]Ready for B42.20!![/h2]
description=[b]Meant to organize our excessive amounts of stuff![/b]
description=
description=Put 99 Nails in a box and now half your base is missing from server chunk rot? Got a shit ton of sheet metal spread among 20 crates because you just can’t fit it into one? No More!
description=
description=[b][i]Item weights for each bundle/box can be adjusted in the Sandbox Options menu if the defaults don’t suit your game :D[/i][/b]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What’s New[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Gas cans keep their fuel.[/b] Bundling gas cans used to quietly empty them - four full cans went in, four empty ones came back out. Packing is now split into [b]Pack Empty Gas Cans[/b] and [b]Pack Full Gas Cans[/b], and a full bundle unpacks into four full cans. A full bundle carries the weight of that fuel too, so it isn’t a free tanker.
description=
description=[b]Changed:[/b] packing now saves weight, not just slots. A pack weighs five sixths of what it holds, which is exactly what vanilla charges for a carton of eggs or a box of canned food, and boxes of nails and screws weigh 40% of theirs to match vanilla’s own box of 100. Every pack in the mod also declares that weight up front now instead of a placeholder, so what the item says and what your inventory says finally agree. All of it is still adjustable per category and per item in Sandbox Options.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] a spoiled or frozen carton could not be opened at all - the game refuses rotten and frozen crafting inputs unless a recipe says otherwise, and the unpack recipes never did. You can now open a rotten carton and get rotten food out of it, and open a frozen one and get frozen food out of it. Packing rotten food is still refused, same as vanilla.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] the Nuts & Bolts box held 50 but was weighed as though it held 10, so it carried a fifth of its real load. It also sat in the Cooking category instead of Material.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] the sacks of coke, the six-packs of cola and diet cola, and the roped gas can bundles were missing from the weight table entirely, so they kept a placeholder weight and ignored the Sandbox reduction sliders. All six now weigh what they hold and have their own per-item slider.
description=
description=[b]Fixed:[/b] the Sack of Sacks pointed at a recipe that doesn’t exist, so double-clicking one did nothing at all. It unpacks now.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What’s Inside[/h2]
description=This mod bundles, boxes, and bags up the clutter so your base (and your sanity) can breathe. A quick taste of what’s covered:
description=
description=[b]Bundles (5/10, rope or sheet rope):[/b] Planks, Sheet Metal, Iron Pipes, Iron Bars, Wire, Small Metal Sheets - plus Gas Cans (4), tied empty or full
description=
description=[b]Boxes & Bags:[/b] Nails, Screws, Gold/Silver Scrap, Pens, Sack of Sacks, Jewelry Pouches
description=
description=[b]Six-Packs:[/b] Ginger Ale, Blueberry, Bubblegum, Grape, Lime, Orange, Pineapple, Strawberry, Variety Pack
description=
description=[b]Scrap Bags:[/b] Aluminum, Brass, Copper, Iron & Steel Scrap, Broken Glass, Wood Charcoal/Splinters, Coke (the blacksmithing kind), and more
description=
description=[b]Parts Boxes:[/b] Iron & Steel Bars/Blocks/Chunks/Pieces, Circular Sawblade, Rake Head, and more
description=
description=[b]Medical Boxes:[/b] Bandages (incl. Dirty… just in case), Painkillers, Sleeping Pills, Caffeine Pills, Alcohol Wipes, Disinfectant, and more
description=
description=[b]Ammo (5/60/240):[/b] B-F, JS-14, M16, M1911, M1A and M9 magazines in Boxes, Cartons and Cases, plus a Carton of Caps. Note: vanilla already boxes and cartons loose rounds, so this rounds out your spare clips instead
description=
description=[b]Seed Pouches (10):[/b] all 55 vanilla seed packet varieties, because your farming stash deserves the same treatment as your nail jar
description=
description=[b]Books & Paperbacks (10):[/b] every genre in the game, plus Leatherbound editions and the Bible (for all you wandering priests out there).
description=
description=[b]Magazines (10):[/b] every topic, old and new print runs, plus Crossword, Wordsearch, HottieZ, and TV Monthly
description=
description=[b]Reading Material (10):[/b] Notebooks, Diaries, Journals, Newspapers (incl. the 4 named city papers), Comics, Catalogs, Phonebooks, Photo/Picture Books, RPG Manuals, Menus, and Loose Paper
description=
description=[b]166 Food Items & 84 Supply Items[/b] via Food Cartons and Supply Boxes from Balsamic Vinegar to Box of Batteries
description=
description=…and many more!!!
description=
description=[b]Stacks go one level deeper.[/b] Cartons pack into Cases, so a hoard that used to bottom out at “a shelf of cartons” collapses one more time. A [b]Food Case[/b] takes four of any of the 166 food cartons, dry goods and produce alike - 48 items in a single slot - and a [b]Magazine Case[/b] takes four magazine cartons, up to 240 mags. Now whenever you siren pull, you can’t complain that you didn’t have enough mags to switch through. Caps were the only ammo in the game that never got a carton, and now they have one. [i][insert cheers here][/i]
description=
description=[b]Perishable Timer:[/b] Food keeps spoiling while it’s packed away, so a carton is storage, not a stasis pod - and that holds however deep you stack it. A packed carton is treated as food in its own right, so it chills in a fridge, freezes solid in a freezer and thaws out again on exactly the same schedule as the loose food beside it, and a frozen carton shows the same frost you’d see on anything else. Age carries all the way up and down the stack, so a case of cartons ticks exactly like a lone carton does, and a rotten carton can’t be laundered into a fresh-looking case. Rotten food can’t be packed in the first place, and the spoilage rate is adjustable in Sandbox Options - packing and unpacking carry how much shelf life is left rather than a raw day count, so 0% really does hold food exactly as you packed it, and 50% really does make it last twice as long.
description=
description=[quote][b]You can’t eat a carton[/b], only open it. [b]PLEASE DO NOT ATTEMPT TO EAT THE CARTON.[/b] Do note that anywhere you’d already explored before updating keeps its old cartons as they were.[/quote]
description=
description=[b]Everything you drop is visible.[/b] All 780 items have a world model, so a dropped pack is an actual pile on the ground instead of thin air. Tied plank, sheet metal, pipe and bar bundles render as real strapped bundles*, and the 10-count ones look bigger than the 5-count ones.
description=
description=[b]Loot:[/b] magazine cartons turn up in gun stores, army and SWAT storage at about a quarter the rate of the boxes, and cap cartons in the Wild West sheriff furniture that already carried the boxes.
