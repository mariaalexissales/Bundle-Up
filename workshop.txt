description=[h2]Ready for B42.20!![/h2]
description=[b]Meant to organize our excessive amounts of stuff![/b]
description=
description=Put 99 Nails in a box and now half your base is missing from server chunk rot? No More!
description=
description=[b][i]Item weights for each bundle/box can be adjusted in the Sandbox Options menu if the defaults don't suit your game :D[/i][/b]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What's New[/h2]
description=[b]Stacks go one level deeper.[/b] Cartons now pack into Cases, so a hoard that used to bottom out at "a shelf of cartons" collapses one more time.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Food Cases (4 cartons):[/b] all 166 food cartons, dry goods and produce alike. That's 48 items in a single slot.
description=[*] [b]Magazine Cartons (12 boxes) & Cases (4 cartons):[/b] your spare B-F, JS-14, M16, M1911, M1A and M9 mags, up to 240 per case.
description=[*] [b]Carton of Caps (12 boxes):[/b] caps were the only ammo in the game that never got a carton. Now they have one.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Everything you drop is finally visible.[/b] 565 of the mod's items had no world model and rendered as thin air when dropped on the ground. Every one of the 780 items now has one.
description=
description=[b]Rope bundles look like rope bundles.[/b] Tied plank, sheet metal, pipe and bar bundles used to render as a single loose plank. They're now actual strapped bundles, and the 10-count ones look bigger than the 5-count ones.
description=
description=[b]The spoilage clock survives nesting.[/b] Food age carries all the way up and down the stack, so a case of cartons keeps ticking exactly like a lone carton does - no laundering a rotten carton into a fresh-looking case.
description=
description=[b]New loot:[/b] magazine cartons turn up in gun stores, army and SWAT storage at about a quarter the rate of the boxes, and cap cartons in the Wild West sheriff furniture that already carried the boxes.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What's Inside[/h2]
description=This mod bundles, boxes, and bags up the clutter so your base (and your sanity) can breathe. A quick taste of what's covered:
description=
description=[b]Bundles (5/10, rope or sheet rope):[/b] Planks, Sheet Metal, Iron Pipes, Iron Bars, Wire, Small Metal Sheets
description=
description=[b]Boxes & Bags:[/b] Nails, Screws, Gold/Silver Scrap, Pens, Sack of Sacks, Jewelry Pouches
description=
description=[b]Six-Packs:[/b] Ginger Ale, Blueberry, Bubblegum, Grape, Lime, Orange, Pineapple, Strawberry, Variety Pack
description=
description=[b]Scrap Bags:[/b] Aluminum, Brass, Copper, Iron & Steel Scrap, Broken Glass, Wood Charcoal/Splinters, and more
description=
description=[b]Parts Boxes:[/b] Iron & Steel Bars/Blocks/Chunks/Pieces, Circular Sawblade, Rake Head, and more
description=
description=[b]Medical Boxes:[/b] Bandages (incl. Dirty), Painkillers, Sleeping Pills, Caffeine Pills, Alcohol Wipes, Disinfectant, and more
description=
description=[b]Ammo (5/60/240):[/b] B-F, JS-14, M16, M1911, M1A and M9 magazines in Boxes, Cartons and Cases, plus a Carton of Caps. Note: vanilla already boxes and cartons loose rounds, so this rounds out your spare clips instead
description=
description=[b]Seed Pouches (10):[/b] all 55 vanilla seed packet varieties, because your farming stash deserves the same treatment as your nail jar
description=
description=[b]Books & Paperbacks (10):[/b] every genre in the game, plus Leatherbound editions and the Bible
description=
description=[b]Magazines (10):[/b] every topic, old and new print runs, plus Crossword, Wordsearch, HottieZ, and TV Monthly
description=
description=[b]Reading Material (10):[/b] Notebooks, Diaries, Journals, Newspapers (incl. the 4 named city papers), Comics, Catalogs, Phonebooks, Photo/Picture Books, RPG Manuals, Menus, and Loose Paper
description=
description=[b]166 Food Items & 84 Supply Items[/b] via Food Cartons and Supply Boxes from Balsamic Vinegar to Box of Batteries - and every food carton now packs again into a Case of four
description=
description=[b]Perishable Timer:[/b] Food keeps spoiling while it's packed away, so a carton is storage, not a stasis pod - and that holds however deep you stack it, cases included. Rotten food can't be packed in the first place, and the spoilage rate is adjustable in Sandbox Options - set it to 0% if you'd rather cartons preserve. Cartons you loot start their clock when the area first generates, same as loose food on the shelf next to them. Do note that fridges and freezers don't slow a carton down, and that anywhere you'd already explored before updating keeps its old cartons as they were.
description=
description=...and many more!!!
