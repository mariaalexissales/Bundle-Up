title=Bundle Up! - A Packing Mod
description=[h1]Bundle Up[/h1]
description=[h2]Ready for B42.20+!![/h2]
description=[b]Meant to organize our excessive amounts of stuff![/b]
description=
description=Put 99 Nails in a box and now half your base is missing from server chunk rot? Got a shit ton of sheet metal spread among 20 crates because you just can’t fit it into one? No More!
description=
description=[b][i]Item weights for each bundle/box can be adjusted in the Sandbox Options menu if the defaults don’t suit your game :D[/i][/b]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What’s Inside[/h2]
description=This mod bundles, boxes, and bags up the clutter so your base (and your sanity) can breathe. [b]780 items[/b] in total, all fully bundleable, boxable, and stackable - a quick taste of what’s covered:
description=
description=[b]Rope Bundles:[/b] Planks, Sheet Metal, Iron Pipes, Iron Bars, Wire, Small Metal Sheets, and Gas Cans - tied in fives or tens with rope or sheet rope, and yes, the gas cans keep their fuel. Pack four full cans and you get four full cans back out, weighing exactly what that fuel weighs, so it’s not a free tanker.
description=
description=[b]Boxes & Bags:[/b] Nails, Screws, Gold/Silver Scrap, Pens, Scrap of every metal under the sun, Parts for pretty much anything you’d find at a hardware store, Sacks of Sacks (yes really), Jewelry Pouches, and Medical Boxes for all your bandages, pills, and wipes.
description=
description=[b]Soda Six-Packs:[/b] Ginger Ale, Blueberry, Bubblegum, Grape, Lime, Orange, Pineapple, Strawberry, and a Variety Pack for the indecisive.
description=
description=[b]Ammo Boxes, Cartons & Cases:[/b] B-F, JS-14, M16, M1911, M1A and M9 magazines, plus a Carton of Caps - the one ammo type vanilla never got around to boxing.
description=
description=[b]Seed Pouches, Books, Magazines & Reading Material:[/b] all 55 vanilla seed varieties, every genre and topic in the game, Leatherbound editions, the Bible, Notebooks, Diaries, Newspapers, Comics, Catalogs, and more.
description=
description=[b]166 Food Items & 84 Supply Items[/b], from Balsamic Vinegar to Box of Batteries, all cartonable and casable.
description=
description=[b]Tiered packing:[/b] we don’t stop at one layer. Cartons pack into Cases, so a hoard that used to bottom out at “a shelf of cartons” collapses one more time. A [b]Food Case[/b] takes four of any of the 166 food cartons - 48 items in a single slot - and a [b]Magazine Case[/b] takes four magazine cartons, up to 240 mags. Never run out of something to read during a siren pull again.
description=
description=[b]Everything you drop is visible.[/b] All 780 items have a world model, so a dropped pack is an actual pile on the ground instead of thin air. Tied plank, sheet metal, pipe and bar bundles render as real strapped bundles*, and the 10-count ones look bigger than the 5-count ones.
description=
description=[b]Perishable Timer:[/b] Food keeps spoiling while it’s packed away, so a carton is storage, not a stasis pod - and that holds however deep you stack it. A packed carton is treated as food in its own right, so it chills in a fridge, freezes solid in a freezer and thaws out again on exactly the same schedule as the loose food beside it, and a frozen carton shows the same frost you’d see on anything else. Age carries all the way up and down the stack, so a case of cartons ticks exactly like a lone carton does, and a rotten carton can’t be laundered into a fresh-looking case. Rotten food can’t be packed in the first place, and the spoilage rate is adjustable in Sandbox Options.
description=
description=[quote][b]You can’t eat a carton[/b], only open it. [b]PLEASE DO NOT ATTEMPT TO EAT THE CARTON.[/b] Do note that anywhere you’d already explored before updating keeps its old cartons as they were.[/quote]
description=
description=[b]Loot:[/b] magazine cartons turn up in gun stores, army and SWAT storage at about a quarter the rate of the boxes, and cap cartons in the Wild West sheriff furniture that already carried the boxes.
description=
description=*Listen, I know that the plank bundle is just a log bundle but now with the plank texture. The budget** didn’t include Blender courses.
description=

description=[h2]Optional: The Packing Panel[/h2]
description=[b]Bundle Up! - UI Add-on[/b] rides along inside this same subscription as a separate, entirely optional mod. Tick it on in the [b]Mods[/b] menu and a button appears next to your crafting one.
description=
description=One window that reads every container in reach - your bags, that crate, the shelf behind you - and lists everything you could pack right now, with a [b]-[/b] / [b]+[/b] / [b]MAX[/b] dial on each row. Tabs up top flip between [b]Bundle[/b] and [b]Unbundle[/b]. [b]Bundle Items[/b] runs what you dialled in, [b]Bundle All[/b] maxes out everything that’s ready, and [b]Stop[/b] is there for when you think better of it halfway through 400 planks. Green tick, you’re good to go. Red, and it tells you what you’re missing and by how much. Bundles go back into the container they came out of.
description=
description=[b][u]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3508537032]NeatUI Framework[/url][/u][/b] by Rocco & Afyrmo - subscribe to it as well or the panel won’t load, and it fails silently, so you’d get no button and no clue why.
description=
description=[i]The base mod needs none of it.[/i] Bundle Up! has no dependencies and isn’t gaining any - leave the add-on off and nothing about your game changes.
