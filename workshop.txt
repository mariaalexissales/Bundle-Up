description=[h1]Bundle Up[/h1]
description=[b]Packs your loot into bundles, boxes, sacks, cartons, cases and crates, and unpacks it exactly as it went in.[/b] For Build 42.20+, single player and multiplayer.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What’s Inside[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]1,700+ packs[/b] covering ~850 vanilla items: rope bundles, boxes, bags, sacks, six-packs and cartons
description=[*][b]Tiered packing:[/b] cartons into Cases, Cases into Crates, up to 192 items in one slot
description=[*][b]No laundering:[/b] part-used, damaged, wet, loaded and rotten items stay out
description=[*][b]Food keeps spoiling[/b] in its carton, on the same clock as the loose food next to it
description=[*][b]Merging:[/b] half-used spools, rolls and bottles merge into whole ones across every container in reach
description=[*][b]Bulk smelting:[/b] iron and steel scrap at the Advanced Furnace
description=[*][b]Seed packets:[/b] relabel an empty one with a pen, pencil, crayon or marker
description=[*][b]Loot:[/b] packs spawn where what’s inside them does
description=[*][b]322 sandbox options[/b] for spawn rates, weights and spoilage
description=[/list]
description=
