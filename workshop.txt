title=Bundle Up! - A Packing Mod
description=[h1]Bundle Up[/h1]
description=[h2]Ready for B42.20+!![/h2]
description=[b]Meant to organize our excessive amounts of stuff![/b]
description=
description=Put 99 Nails in a box and now half your base is missing from server chunk rot? Got a shit ton of sheet metal spread among 20 crates because you just can’t fit it into one? No More!
description=
description=[b][i]Item weights for each bundle/box can be adjusted in the Sandbox Options menu if the defaults don’t suit your game :D[/i][/b]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What’s Inside[/h2]
description=This mod bundles, boxes and bags up the clutter so your base (and your sanity) can breathe. [b]1000+ items covered[/b] across old and new B42 stock - rope bundles, boxes, bags and sacks, six-packs, cartons and cases - all of it packable and stackable.
description=
description=[i]Packing never launders an item - part-used, damaged, wet, loaded or rotten stock stays out, and what goes in comes back out exactly as it was, right down to the colour of the wine.[/i]
description=
description=[b]Tiered packing:[/b] cartons pack into Cases, so a hoard that used to bottom out at “a shelf of cartons” collapses one more time. A [b]Food Case[/b] takes four of any of the 166 food cartons - 48 items in a single slot - and a [b]Magazine Case[/b] takes four magazine cartons, up to 240 mags.
description=
description=[b]Everything you drop is visible.[/b] Every last one of them has a world model, so a dropped pack is an actual pile on the ground instead of thin air. Tied bundles render as real strapped bundles*, and the 10-count ones look bigger than the 5-count ones.
description=
description=[b]Perishable Timer:[/b] food keeps spoiling while it’s packed away, so a carton is storage, not a stasis pod. A packed carton is food in its own right - it chills, freezes and thaws on the same schedule as the loose food beside it, frost and all - and age carries up and down the stack, so a rotten carton can’t be laundered into a fresh-looking case. Spoilage rate is adjustable in Sandbox Options.
description=
description=[quote][b]You can’t eat a carton[/b], only open it. [b]PLEASE DO NOT ATTEMPT TO EAT THE CARTON.[/b] Do note that anywhere you’d already explored before updating keeps its old cartons as they were.[/quote]
description=
description=[b]Loot:[/b] everything spawns where you’d expect it - magazine cartons in gun stores and army storage, cap cartons in the Wild West sheriff furniture, hides in leather crates and tanneries, ingots and ore in metalwork and blacksmith crates, building supplies in masonry and gardening crates, and the drink packs behind bars and in liquor crates.
description=
description=*Listen, I know that the plank bundle is just a log bundle but now with the plank texture. The budget** didn’t include Blender courses.
description=
description=** There is no budget/funding. But if you’d like to, support see below!
description=
description=[b]Tidying part-used stock:[/b] half-empty spools, rolls and bottles merge back into whole ones. The base game only ever looks at what is loose in your own inventory, so a bag of part-picked aramid thread offers you nothing at all - now [b]Add to[/b] and [b]Consolidate all[/b] read every container in reach, and another 98 supplies the base game never labelled get the option too. Nothing is lost in the pour, and what survives goes back where it came from.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Optional: The Packing Panel[/h2]
description=[b]Bundle Up! - UI Add-on[/b] rides along inside this same subscription as a separate, entirely optional mod. Tick it on in the [b]Mods[/b] menu and a button appears next to your crafting one.
description=
description=One window that reads every container in reach - your bags, that crate, the shelf behind you - and lists everything you could pack right now, with a [b]-[/b] / [b]+[/b] / [b]MAX[/b] dial on each row. Tabs flip between [b]Bundle[/b], [b]Unbundle[/b] and [b]Merge[/b], [b]Bundle All[/b] maxes out everything that’s ready, and [b]Stop[/b] is there for when you think better of it halfway through 400 planks. Bundles go back into the container they came out of.
description=
description=[b][u]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3508537032]NeatUI Framework[/url][/u][/b] by Rocco & Afyrmo - subscribe to it as well or the panel won’t load, and it fails silently, so you’d get no button and no clue why.
description=
description=[i]The base mod needs none of it.[/i] Bundle Up! has no dependencies and isn’t gaining any - leave the add-on off and nothing about your game changes.
